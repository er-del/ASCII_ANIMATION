SUMMIT

On a mountain, a seed grows into a tree. An apple ripens and falls, and a black hole peels it apart in a spiral. Then everything explodes into a new universe, the Milky Way re-forms, and the camera dives back through the solar system and Earth to the mountain. Sound is generated live. Warning: it contains bright flashes and camera shake; the Calm mode softens them.

Mountain, lake, forest and one small tree. Made from text.
